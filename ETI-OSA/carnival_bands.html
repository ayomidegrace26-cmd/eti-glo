What is a Carnival Band?

Carnival bands are the beating heart of the Eti-Osa Carnival. These competing performance groups each represent a Royal Family of Eti-Osa, bringing their unique history, colors, and energy to the streets. They march in the two spectacular Grand Parades on December 19 and December 25, turning the city into a vibrant display of cultural pride.

Bands compete fiercely on costume design, choreography, crowd energy, and cultural representation. The band that delivers the most captivating performance and best embodies the spirit of Eti-Osa is crowned the prestigious "Carnival Band of the Year" — a title that brings immense honor to their representing Royal House.

Band of the Year Award

The ultimate prize of the Eti-Osa Carnival. The Band of the Year award recognizes the Royal Band that best exemplifies the spirit, creativity, and cultural depth of our community during the Grand Parades.

Judging Criteria

  • Costume & Design: Creativity, execution, and visual impact.

  • Choreography & Performance: Synchronization, skill, and storytelling through movement.

  • Cultural Representation: Authentic portrayal of Eti-Osa heritage and royal lineage.

  • Crowd Energy: Ability to engage, excite, and move the festival attendees.

The Grand Prize

The winning band receives the prestigious Carnival Trophy, a cash prize of ₦5,000,000 for their Royal House, and the honor of leading the parade the following year.
