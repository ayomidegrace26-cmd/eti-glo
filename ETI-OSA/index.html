"14 days. One city. A carnival unlike anything Lagos has ever seen."

Experience the grandeur of Eti-Osa. From the majestic Royal Durbar to the vibrant street parades and the enchanting floating concert, immerse yourself in a world-class celebration of African heritage. Join us as we transform Lagos into a spectacular canvas of culture, art, and unity.
