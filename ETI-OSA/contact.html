Frequently Asked
Questions

When is the Eti-Osa Carnival 2026?
The Eti-Osa Carnival 2026 is scheduled to take place over 14 days in December 2026. Exact dates will be announced shortly. Please subscribe to our newsletter for the latest updates.

How do I buy tickets?
Tickets can be purchased directly through our website via the "Events & Tickets" page. We offer various tiers including Standard, VIP, and VVIP access. Early bird tickets will be available soon.

How do I join a Royal Carnival Band?
You can apply to join a band by visiting the "Carnival Bands" section of our website. Select your preferred band and fill out the registration form. Note that spaces are limited and registration fees apply.

How do I register as a vendor?
Vendor registration is available on the "Vendors" page. We have opportunities for both the Trade Fair and Event Day vending. You will need to submit an application and necessary documentation for approval.

Is the carnival suitable for children?
Yes, the Eti-Osa Carnival is a family-friendly event. We have specific days and zones dedicated entirely to children's activities, cultural education, and family entertainment.

How do I become a sponsor?
Please visit our "Sponsorship" page to review our available tiers ranging from Festival Donor to Royal Patron. You can submit an enquiry form there, or contact our partnership team directly via the form on this page.

Will there be parking?
Yes, designated parking areas will be available for attendees. VIP and VVIP ticket holders will have access to premium parking zones. We also highly recommend using our official park-and-ride shuttle services.

Is the carnival free?
The carnival features a mix of free public events (such as the main street parade) and ticketed exclusive events (such as the Royal Banquet and main concerts). Check the event schedule for specific details.

How can I volunteer?
We welcome volunteers! Please select "Volunteer" from the subject dropdown in the contact form above, and tell us a bit about your skills and availability. Our volunteer coordinator will be in touch.

Are there group ticket discounts?
Yes, we offer special rates for corporate bookings and large groups (10+ people). Please contact our ticketing team using the form above for a custom quote.
