About the Eti-Osa Carnival

Lagos State's Premier Cultural Festival

Our Story

The Eti-Osa Carnival was born from a profound desire to celebrate the rich, multifaceted heritage of one of Lagos' most dynamic regions. Rooted in the waterfront identity and royal lineage of the Eti-Osa people, our festival is more than an event; it is a living testament to our history.

"Built on heritage. Designed for the world."

Historically, Eti-Osa has been a melting pot of cultures, a vibrant coastal community where trade, tradition, and innovation have always intersected. The carnival brings this legacy to life, transforming the streets and waterways into a spectacular canvas of color, music, and dance.

From the majestic Royal Durbar to the rhythmic beats of the street parades, every element of the carnival is meticulously curated to honor our ancestors while embracing the future. It is a celebration of unity, a showcase of artistic brilliance, and an invitation to the world to experience the true spirit of Lagos.

Legacy

The Heritage of Eti-Osa

Eti-Osa's identity is inextricably linked to its coastal geography. The waterways have not only shaped its economy but also its cultural expressions, from traditional boat regattas to the vibrant festivals that celebrate the spirits of the water.

At the heart of this heritage are the Royal Families of Eti-Osa, custodians of centuries-old traditions. Their presence and patronage infuse the carnival with an authenticity and grandeur that cannot be replicated elsewhere.

Royal Lineage: Honoring the historic houses of Elegushi, Oniru, Ojomu, and Ikateland.

Coastal Culture: Celebrating the maritime traditions that define our waterfront identity.

Artistic Expression: Showcasing indigenous music, dance, and intricate craftsmanship.

"To celebrate, preserve, and project the heritage of Eti-Osa to Nigeria and the world — one carnival at a time."

Our Mission

To preserve and promote the cultural identity of Eti-Osa through a sustainable and inclusive festival that fosters community pride and global cultural exchange.

Our Vision

To become Africa's premier cultural destination, recognized globally for excellence in heritage preservation and artistic celebration.
